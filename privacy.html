Privacy Policy

Devan Placements, operated by Devan Enterprises ("we", "us"), is an overseas manpower recruitment and placement consultancy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

  • Candidate details: full name, mobile / WhatsApp number, email address, current location, preferred country, job category, qualification, work experience, job role and your resume or CV.
  • Job applications and enquiries: the job you applied or asked about, and any message you send.
  • Employer enquiries: company name, contact person, country, email, phone and manpower requirements.
  • Contact messages: name, email, phone and your message.
  • Technical data: the page address from which a form was sent. We do not use advertising cookies on this website.

2. Why we use it

  • To review your profile and match you with suitable overseas vacancies.
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your application or enquiry.
  • To share your profile with overseas employers or their representatives when you are being considered for a vacancy.
  • To respond to employer and general enquiries and to improve our service.

3. Consent

When you submit a form you consent to us processing the information you provide for the purposes above. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us (see section 8).

4. Where your information is stored

Form data and uploaded resumes are stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive accounts controlled by Devan Enterprises. Google acts as our service provider. Access is limited to our recruitment team.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with overseas employers, their recruitment partners and authorities where needed for a recruitment, and with service providers who help us run the website and our communications. Employers abroad may be subject to different data protection laws.

6. How long we keep it

We keep candidate profiles and resumes for as long as needed for recruitment purposes, and then delete them or on request, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online system is completely secure. Please do not send documents such as passport copies through the website forms; we will tell you when and how to share them safely.

8. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email us at or call .

9. Children

Our services are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Third-party links

This website links to services such as WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
